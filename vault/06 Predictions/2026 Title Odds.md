## How it works
1. Every remaining game is played 10,000 times with the final model. Each simulated season also
   nudges every team's strength up or down (about 3.1 points), because
   we're never sure exactly how good a team is. Single games add noise of about
   15.4 points.
2. Conference title games are set from the simulated standings.
3. A committee emulator, trained on the real CFP rankings 2014-2025, ranks the teams
   ([[EXP-004 Committee Emulator]]).
4. The 12-team field is built with the 2026 rules and the bracket is played out.

Assumptions:
- 2026 CFP: 12 teams; ACC/Big Ten/Big 12/SEC champions + highest-ranked team from the other six conferences + Notre Dame if top 12; seeds follow the ranking.
- Conference title game = top two by conference record (ties: overall record, then strength). Sun Belt = East winner vs West winner.
- Pac-12 flex games use the league's projected matchups and don't count in standings.
- North Dakota State and Sacramento State (first FBS season) are ineligible for conference titles and the CFP.
- Team states are frozen at today's data; uncertainty enters through tau and game noise.
